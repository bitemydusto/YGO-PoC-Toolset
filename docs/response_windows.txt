Stored in the 2nd word of the condition param
Value = (word & 0xFC0) >> 6

0x5,0x6: normal/flip summon (unknown order)
0x7: special summon
0x20, 0x21, 0x22: summon attempt, spell/trap activation (unknown order)

For effect response, check if param2 is not null and check for the following:

-Location (param2[1] & 0x3E)
  -field: <= 0x14
  -hand:  0x16

-Status (higher bits of param2[1], e.g. param2[1 & 0x3000)
